§1 Problem statement

The problem you'd hire me to solve

Most teams can now demo something impressive with AI. Far fewer can turn it into a product that earns trust, fits a real workflow, and moves a business metric.

That gap, between "the model can do it" and "customers rely on it," is where I do my best work.
